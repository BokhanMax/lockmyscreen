LockMyScreen is a tiny Windows timer that locks your computer, or just reminds you, when time is up. Start a timer, keep working, and when the time runs out the screen locks, just like pressing Win+L.

How it works

No setup, no accounts. The widget sits in the corner of your screen, out of the way.

Set the time

Type the minutes into the widget, or pick hours, minutes and seconds in the main window.

Keep working

The countdown runs in the widget and the tray icon. Your work isn't interrupted.

Time's up

Windows locks the screen (or shows a reminder), and you remember what you were waiting for.

Features

Small on purpose. Everything a kitchen timer for your PC needs, and nothing more.

Lock or remind

Lock the computer with the standard Windows lock screen, or show a big always-on-top reminder that beeps until you close it.

After a while, or at a time

Count down a duration with quick presets (5, 15, 30 min, 1 h), or fire at a specific time of day.

Smart repeat

The next countdown starts only after you unlock the PC, so time spent away from your desk doesn't count. Great for regular breaks.

Desktop widget

A compact always-on-top bar with a live countdown. Drag it anywhere, make it semi-transparent, or hide it.

Three languages

English, Ukrainian and Russian. Picked from your Windows language and switchable at any time.

Tiny and dependency-free

A single small exe built on .NET Framework 4.8, which ships with Windows. It lives in the tray and remembers your settings.

Install

Download LockMyScreen-Setup-<version>.exe from the latest release and run it.

The installer

  • No admin rights: installs for the current user only
  • Optional start with Windows and desktop shortcut
  • Upgrades in place and keeps your settings
  • Uninstall from Settings → Apps

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11
  • 64-bit (x64) or 32-bit (x86): runs natively on both
  • Nothing else: .NET Framework 4.8 is already part of Windows
Seeing “Windows protected your PC”? The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you on first run. Click More info → Run anyway.

Speaks your language

The app and the installer both follow your Windows display language.
